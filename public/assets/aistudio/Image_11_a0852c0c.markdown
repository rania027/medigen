## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a haute-clinical paradigm for diagnostic intelligence and clinical decision support. Moving intentionally away from sterile, cold blue-dominated medical interfaces, the visual philosophy marries high-fashion editorial tension with serene, organic warmth. 

The aesthetic is built for clinicians, diagnostic researchers, and medical directors who demand surgical clarity without visual fatigue. High-contrast typography provides authoritative, editorial gravitas, while soft organic contours, gentle tactile surfaces, and warm ivory backdrops evoke calm precision, emotional safety, and unyielding diagnostic prestige.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-stakes diagnostic decision-making in biological balance and serenity:

- **Primary (`#3A6364` - Deep Mineral Teal):** Serves as the primary anchor for decisive actions, active clinical states, high-priority vectors, and focused navigation chrome.
- **Secondary (`#8A9A86` - Sage Laurel):** Used for contextual validation, stable physiological ranges, secondary actions, and environmental grounding.
- **Tertiary (`#7C6A96` - Muted Lavender Core):** Governs algorithmic recommendations, AI insights, and differential diagnostics.
- **Neutral (`#2B2D42` - Dark Charcoal):** Yields high-contrast, accessible typography and clinical iconography without the stark brutality of pure black.

### Supporting Diagnostic Accents & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FDFBF7` (Warm Ivory Off-White), an anti-glare, comforting background engineered for prolonged clinical review sessions.
- **Soft Mint Accent (`#D1E7DD`):** Low-urgency status indications, verified vitals, and benign diagnostic pathways.
- **Muted Coral Accent (`#F8D7DA`):** Softened triage warnings, contraindication alerts, and critical lab values that inform rather than panic.
- **Neutral Wash:** `#F4F0E8` for subtle inset wells, grouped table rows, and secondary containers.

## Typography

The typography system pairs extreme editorial elegance with crystal-clear legibility:

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers a high-contrast serif structure for patient case titles, clinical score highlights, and top-tier section headers. It brings bespoke sophistication, dignifying patient documentation into an elevated artifact.
- **Interface & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Offers geometric clarity with humanist warmth. Its wide aperture, distinct counters, and rounded terminals guarantee rapid legibility across dense diagnostic tables, multi-parameter lab outputs, and pharmacological dosages.
- **Numerical Treatment:** All clinical values, vitals, and timestamps rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` use tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to guarantee instantaneous vertical scanning during time-critical consultations.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts in this design system follow a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to manage heavy clinical information density without visual crowding:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. The workspace organizes into a persistent, narrow primary navigation rail (80px), a flexible primary diagnostic feed (7–8 columns), and an intelligent decision support panel (4–5 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Decision support folds into collapsible contextual drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Diagnostic cards stack sequentially with sticky bottom action controls.

Vertical rhythm is governed by the spacing scale. Tightly related diagnostic metrics rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm`, component interiors leverage `space-md` and `space-lg`, and major clinical segments partition using `space-xl` and `space-xxl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons cold, synthetic dropshadows in favor of warm, diffuse ambient elevation and layered tonal stratification:

- **Base Canvas:** The foundation sits at `#FDFBF7`. Sub-sections and table regions are recessed using a subtle tonal tier of `#F4F0E8` with no shadow.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Panels):** Elevated cards use `#FFFFFF` rested atop the warm canvas, bounded by a feather-light ghost border (`rgba(58, 99, 100, 0.08)`).
- **Ambient Shadow Character:** Where depth is required (such as contextual popovers, active decision flyouts, or floating dosage calculators), shadows are tinted with the seed color `#3A6364`:
  - *Low Elevation (Cards on hover, Select menus):* `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(58, 99, 100, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.03)`
  - *High Elevation (Diagnostic Drawers, Modals):* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(58, 99, 100, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.04)`
- **Backdrop Blur:** Modal underlays utilize a subtle warm diffusion: `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.8)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` to maintain context without cognitive noise.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-soft, organic geometry (`roundedness: 3`). By implementing pill-shaped contours, generous corner radii, and continuous curvatures, the interface counters the anxiety often induced by clinical tools:

- **Micro Controls & Badges:** Use full pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px` or minimum `1rem`) to present data points, lab status flags, and filter tags as tactile capsules.
- **Buttons & Form Fields:** Default to a rounded `1rem` (16px), giving touch and mouse targets an inviting, humanized appearance.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Leverage `rounded-lg` (`2rem` / 32px), creating fluid, pebble-like perimeters around dense diagnostic outputs.
- **Major Modals & Slide-out Trays:** Utilize `rounded-xl` (`3rem` / 48px) along active perimeter corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#3A6364` with `#FFFFFF` text. Fully rounded (`1rem` to pill). High-touch padding (`0.75rem 1.75rem`). Hover transitions slightly brighten to `#4A7C7D` accompanied by a gentle upward spring (`transform: translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined button with a `1.5px` border in `#8A9A86`, `#3A6364` label, and transparent background. On hover, fills with a translucent wash (`rgba(138, 154, 134, 0.12)`).
- **Tertiary / Clinical AI Trigger:** Lavender wash (`rgba(124, 106, 150, 0.15)`) with `#7C6A96` text, signaling algorithmic inference and suggested differential diagnoses.

### Clinical Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.375rem 0.875rem` with uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- **Stable / Normal:** Soft Mint background (`#D1E7DD`) with deep teal-slate text (`#284B4C`).
- **Investigating / AI Insight:** Lavender background (`#E2D9F3`) with muted purple text (`#52436B`).
- **Attention Required:** Muted Peach/Coral background (`#F8D7DA`) with deep mahogany text (`#6A2B33`).

### Cards & Diagnostic Panels
- Background `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px organic border in `rgba(58, 99, 100, 0.08)`.
- Internal padding: `1.5rem` (mobile) to `2rem` (desktop).
- Header sections feature a dual-font interplay: the case category in `label-sm` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) followed by the diagnostic title in `headline-md` (`Bodoni Moda`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Pill or soft-rounded (`1rem`) with an off-white fill (`#F4F0E8`), a 1px border in `rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.1)`, and body text in `#2B2D42`. On focus, the field shifts to pure `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `2px` focus ring in `#3A6364`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic checks (`0.5rem` radius for checkboxes; full circle for radios). Inactive states use a warm gray outline; checked states fill with `#3A6364` accompanied by crisp white iconography.

### Clinical Lists & Observation Streams
- Row items present as floating pill-shaped or soft-rect capsules over the warm canvas.
- Alternating or highlighted findings leverage subtle inset backgrounds (`#F4F0E8`) rather than stark grid divider lines to minimize visual clutter.
